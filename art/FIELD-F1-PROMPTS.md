# Field F1: front-facing encounters

Built-in image generation. Six region atlases, 1536×1024, 3 columns × 2 rows. Runtime WebP copies preserve the generated artwork. Each of the 36 complete creatures faces the viewer and is centered in its own environment. UI and gameplay numbers remain live code.

Common prompt: premium detailed realistic fantasy RPG encounter atlas, one complete front-facing monster per equal square cell, sharp fur/stone/armor, matching regional scenery, no player characters, no text or UI. Independent encounters, all limbs and weapons within each cell.

Ordered cells:
- Grassland: emerald slime, gray wolf, giant boar, forest goblin, root/stone guardian, jade forest spirit. Ancient forest, moss and shafts of light.
- Desert: sand scorpion, hyena, mummy, sandstorm spirit, reptilian tyrant, jackal pharaoh king. Warm dark desert atmosphere and ancient ornamentation.
- Snow: ice-fanged wolf, snow troll, frost witch, frozen giant, armored bear lord, crystalline frost sovereign. Icy cliffs and snow.
- Volcano: ash wyvern, lava knight, magma shaman, obsidian golem, volcanic demon lord, crowned flame sovereign. Lava ruins and embers.
- Sky: dawn gatekeeper, galaxy knight, cloud sage, radiant seraph, starlight judge, heavenly emperor. Marble sky temples and clouds.
- Abyss: abyss watcher, void knight, oblivion priest, riftbreaker, abyss lord, void ruler. Ruined black temples and violet rifts.

Rendering: lazy-load only requested regional sheets, cache scenes and thumbnails. Confirmed damage triggers short localized impact and slash; critical hits add a larger cross slash. Reduced motion suppresses displacement. No field player character, equipment or aura rendering.
