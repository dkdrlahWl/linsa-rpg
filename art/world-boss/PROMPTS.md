# WB1 artwork

Built-in image generation, referenced to the game's existing heroes and Kael/Serin costume sheets. No aura/pet renderer is used by the world-boss scene. Exact tile geometry, warning circles, buttons, health bars and tombstones are code-native, not baked into an image.

## ooo-v1.png

Production game background, wide landscape. Reference: the previously approved horned dragon/eclipsed fortress concept and existing semi-realistic game heroes. Enormous dark armored horned dragon upper body/head and two clawed hands looming above a low stone parapet; burning ruined fortress and orange eclipse behind. Head centered, claws lower left/right, straight-on camera. Bottom fades into dark stone. No people, UI, text, grid, health bars, or watermarks. Detailed dark-fantasy painted game style, not cartoon/chibi.

## heroes-back-v1.png

Four equal-width columns in one horizontal row. Full-body motionless characters seen directly from behind, facing the boss/north. Preserve identities and clothes: black-haired man in brown leather; loose silver-haired woman in brown leather; short silver-haired Kael in charcoal cape; silver-ponytail Serin in ornate charcoal coat. Same scale, aligned feet, separated cells. Semi-realistic game rendering. No aura, glow, pets, attack action, UI, or text. Background removal was requested from the built-in generator; the runtime additionally clips to individual silhouette paths and strokes the local player's body in green.
